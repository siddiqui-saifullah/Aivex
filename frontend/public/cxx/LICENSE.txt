In-browser C++ compiler

- Clang/LLVM 21.1.4 compiled to WebAssembly by the YoWASP project
  (@yowasp/clang, https://yowasp.org, ISC license). Copied here at build time
  by scripts/prepare-cxx.mjs.
- LLVM, Clang, libc++, libc++abi and libunwind: Apache License 2.0 with LLVM
  Exceptions (https://llvm.org/LICENSE.txt). libc++, libc++abi and libunwind
  in sysroot.tar.gz are rebuilt with WebAssembly exceptions by
  tools/cxx/build.sh.
- wasi-libc (in sysroot.tar.gz): MIT, Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception and
  others (https://github.com/WebAssembly/wasi-libc/blob/main/LICENSE).
